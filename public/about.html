About and safety

What this site is, where the information comes from, and what to do when severe weather threatens.

About

West Central Ohio Weather Center is an independent weather information project for Bellefontaine, Logan County and surrounding communities. It combines official National Weather Service data with our own forecast discussions and weather education.

Sources and disclaimer

  • Forecasts, observations and alerts come from the National Weather Service API. They are shown as received and can be delayed or unavailable.
  • Radar images come from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet NEXRAD mosaic.
  • Blog posts, forecast discussions and graphics labeled as ours are the opinion of this project. They are not official warnings.

West Central Ohio Weather Center is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or part of the National Weather Service, NOAA or any government agency. Follow official warnings and the instructions of local emergency officials. Do not use this site as your only source of warnings.

Tornado safety

  • Go to the lowest floor, in an interior room away from windows (a basement or storm shelter is best).
  • Mobile and manufactured homes are not safe in a tornado. Move to a sturdy building before the storm arrives.
  • Do not wait to see or hear a tornado. Act when a warning is issued.

Flooding

  • Never drive through flooded roads. Turn around, don't drown.
  • Flash flooding can occur quickly on small creeks and in low-lying areas.

Winter weather

  • Avoid travel during ice storms and blizzard conditions. Keep a winter kit in your vehicle.
  • Use generators and heaters outdoors or with proper ventilation to prevent carbon monoxide poisoning.
