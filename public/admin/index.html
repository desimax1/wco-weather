Editor sign-in

Publishing is handled by Pages CMS, a free editor that signs in with your GitHub account and saves your posts, forecast discussion and images straight into this website's repository. This page is only a launch pad. There is no password on this website, and nothing here can be edited without signing in to GitHub.

Open the editor (Pages CMS)

After you save, the site rebuilds automatically and your changes appear in about 1 to 2 minutes.

What you can do there

  • Forecast Discussion: edit the discussion shown on the Forecast page.
  • Blog Posts: write, save as draft, publish, categorize and add images.
  • Maps and Graphics: upload maps with captions.
