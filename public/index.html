Know the difference

Watch

Conditions are favorable for dangerous weather. Stay alert, have a plan, and keep a way to receive warnings.

Warning

Dangerous weather is happening or about to happen. Take action now. For a tornado warning, move to the lowest interior room away from windows.

Get warnings overnight

Use a NOAA Weather Radio and enable Wireless Emergency Alerts on your phone. Do not rely on a website alone. More safety information
