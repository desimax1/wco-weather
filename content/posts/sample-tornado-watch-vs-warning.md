## Watch

A **tornado watch** means conditions are favorable for tornadoes to develop. It covers a large area for several hours. Review your plan and keep a way to get warnings.

## Warning

A **tornado warning** means a tornado is happening or is about to happen. It covers a small area for a short time. Take shelter immediately in the lowest interior room away from windows.

Learn more from the [National Weather Service safety pages](https://www.weather.gov/safety/).
