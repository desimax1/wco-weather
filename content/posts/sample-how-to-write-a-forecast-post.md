## Structure that works

Lead with the most important point. Residents want to know: do I need to change my plans today?

1. Start with a one-sentence bottom line.
2. Give the timing for any rain, storms or snow.
3. Say how confident you are.
4. Link to official sources such as the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/iln/).

Save the post as a **draft** first if you want to finish it later.
